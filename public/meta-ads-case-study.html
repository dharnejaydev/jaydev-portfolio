01 — Background

The Problem

During my internship at DigitizeBrand Hub, two active Meta Ads lead generation campaigns were running but underperforming — high cost per lead, limited reach, and stagnant results. My task was to assist in diagnosing what was wrong and support the optimization process.

My role as intern: I supported the campaign analysis, helped identify audience and budget issues, assisted in restructuring ad sets, and monitored performance post-optimization using Meta Ads Manager. All decisions were reviewed with the team lead.

03 — What We Did

Optimization Strategy

The optimization covered four core areas: audience targeting, budget scaling, attribution setup, and ad set structure.

  • 1

    Audience Analysis and Refinement

    Reviewed existing audience targeting — identified broad or mismatched audience segments that were eating budget without converting. Helped narrow targeting to higher-intent audiences based on interests and behaviors relevant to the offer.

  • 2

    Attribution Window Fix

    The CNDT campaign had no attribution setting configured, meaning Meta wasn't optimizing correctly for leads. We set a 7-day click attribution window on both campaigns, giving the algorithm proper data to learn from and optimize delivery toward converting users.

  • 3

    Budget Scaling with Performance Guardrails

    After identifying that the campaigns were limited by budget (not performance), we increased daily spend from ₹200 to ₹350 — a 75% increase. This was done gradually to avoid resetting the learning phase and disrupting Meta's delivery algorithm.

  • 4

    Campaign Objective and Bid Strategy Review

    Confirmed both campaigns were running on "Highest Volume" bid strategy, appropriate for maximizing leads within budget. Validated that the campaign objective was set to Lead Generation and that the Meta lead form was properly connected and submitting correctly.

  • 5

    Performance Monitoring Post-Launch

    Tracked daily metrics in Ads Manager after changes went live. Monitored CPL, reach, and impressions over the following days to confirm the algorithm had exited the learning phase and results were stabilizing at the improved level.

04 — Key Learnings

What I Learned

🎯

Attribution matters

A missing attribution window was silently hurting the algorithm's optimization. Small setup details have large downstream effects.

📈

Budget scaling strategy

Increasing budget too fast resets the learning phase. Gradual scaling preserves algorithm momentum and avoids CPL spikes.

👥

Audience quality over size

Narrowing to intent-matched audiences reduced wasted impressions and brought CPL down significantly despite higher reach overall.

📊

Read data before acting

The instinct to change creatives first was wrong — the issue was structural (attribution + audience), not creative fatigue.
